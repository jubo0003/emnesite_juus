ABOUT

Born and based in Copenhagen, Denmark, Juus is a contemporary jewellery brand inspired by lujuus - the Finnish word for strength. Our name is a reflection of what we stand for: timeless design and honest, resilient materials.

We craft unisex jewellery from 100% recycled sterling silver, giving new life to existing materials instead of drawing from the earth. Our pieces are designed to last — timeless in form, durable in function, and made to be worn every day.

Sustainability for us is not a trend — it’s a responsibility. We work in small batches to reduce waste, use low-impact packaging, and partner with local craftsmen to keep our production close and our footprint low. Every detail matters — from the material we choose to the pace at which we create.

We shape each piece with a sculptural, minimal approach. Rooted in Scandinavian design values, Juus embraces simplicity with substance—where form follows feeling, and every detail is intentional.

Our pieces are made for everyday wear, designed to move and age with you. Raw yet refined. Bold yet understated. They’re here to endure.
